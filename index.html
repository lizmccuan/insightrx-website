The Decision Gap

The dashboards end where
the hard part begins.

Once the data is available, someone still has to connect the signals, decide what matters, determine whether the evidence is strong enough, and choose the right next step.

01
Interpret
What is actually happening?
Metrics from different platforms have to be understood in context before they become useful insights.
02
Prioritize
What deserves attention first?
Not every change in performance should become a marketing priority.
03
Verify
Is the evidence strong enough?
Before acting, marketers need to know whether a finding is supported by enough reliable data.
04
Act
What should we do next?
Even after an opportunity is identified, someone still has to determine the right action.

The opportunity for AI: shorten the distance between data and a decision without sacrificing accuracy, trust, or human judgment.

Built for Trust

Faster Analysis
Without Blind Trust

InsightRx is designed to increase speed without hiding uncertainty or giving AI unrestricted control over marketing decisions.

Missing ≠ Zero
Unavailable metrics remain unknown rather than being converted into zero.
No Invented Data
InsightRx does not create URLs, conversions, clicks, or metrics that are not present in the source data.
Evidence Before Claims
Recommendations must be supported by the available marketing evidence.
Sample Size Matters
Small signals are treated differently from strong, high-volume evidence.
Evaluation Layer
Generated execution output can be reviewed for quality before use.
Human in the Loop
The marketer reviews evidence and approves actions before implementation.
The goal is not full autonomy. It is better decision support.

The Three Layers

SEO. AEO. GEO.

A stronger search presence starts with all three.

Each layer plays a unique role in how brands are discovered, understood, and recommended across traditional and AI-powered search experiences.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

Help people find your content in traditional search results.

  • ✓Search visibility
  • ✓Query performance
  • ✓Technical SEO
  • ✓Content relevance
  • ✓Internal linking
AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Make content easier for answer engines to understand and use when responding to questions.

  • ✓Clear question-and-answer structure
  • ✓Concise definitions
  • ✓FAQs
  • ✓Structured content
  • ✓Entity clarity
GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

Improve how clearly a brand, topic, or source can be understood within generative search environments.

  • ✓Authority signals
  • ✓Machine-readable content
  • ✓Entity relationships
  • ✓Source clarity
  • ✓Brand consistency
💡The key idea: Traditional SEO still matters. AEO and GEO build on that foundation rather than replacing it.
How Discovery Is Changing

The same question.
A different journey.

People are still asking the same questions, but the way they find and consume information is evolving. AI-generated answers are becoming part of the search experience, and agent-based tools may increasingly research and compare on a user's behalf.

Traditional Search
Search → Results → Website
Users search, review results, and choose which links to visit.
→
AI-Assisted Search
Question → Synthesized Answer → Sources
Users ask a question, AI generates an answer, and may cite or reference relevant sources.
→
Emerging Agent Experiences
Research → Compare → Assist
AI systems may increasingly research, compare, and assist with decisions on a user's behalf.
📊Discovery is changing. The underlying need for clear, authoritative, and helpful information is not.

Reflection

From Campaign Thinking
to Systems Thinking

Building InsightRx in a production environment changed how I think about marketing, AI, and the intersection of both.

How my thinking shifted
Campaign Thinking → Systems Thinking

Shifted from “what should I do?” to “how do I architect a system that answers that automatically?” Every strategy now looks like an optimization problem waiting to be built.

Prompt Engineering → AI System Design

Prompts alone are not enough. Production AI requires grounded data, structured outputs, agent orchestration, and evaluation frameworks. Prompts are one component of a larger system.

SEO → SEO + AEO + GEO

After auditing real brands against ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews, it became clear: brands are already winning and losing in AI-mediated discovery — most don’t know it yet.

Strategy → Strategy + Engineering

The best strategy is only as useful as the system that can execute it. The best pipeline is worthless without real strategic questions. InsightRx forced me to hold both simultaneously.

What production AI actually requires
Prompts are necessary — but not sufficient.
InsightRx required all seven layers to produce reliable, grounded output.
Grounded Data Structured Outputs Agent Orchestration Evaluation Frameworks Data Normalization Reliable Workflows Prompt Engineering ↑ one of seven
Key takeaways
🔗
Grounding is the hardest part of AI

Getting agents to produce useful output is easy. Getting them grounded in live, clean, normalized data — handling authentication, rate limits, and schema mismatches — is where most AI projects fail.

data_grounding
✦
AEO and GEO are already real

Brands are already winning and losing in AI-mediated discovery — they just don’t know it yet. The window to build early advantage is narrow, and InsightRx is designed to identify it.

aeo_geo_visibility
🎯
Strategy and engineering are inseparable

InsightRx forced me to hold both simultaneously — strategic marketing questions grounding an AI pipeline, an AI pipeline making strategy executable. That intersection is where I want to work.

systems_thinking
